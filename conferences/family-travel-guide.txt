FAMILY TRAVEL GUIDE — Conferences with wife + 2yo kid
======================================================

Based on: flight time from Barcelona, kid-friendliness, things for
family while at the conference, weather, and travel coverage.


TOP TIER
--------

Athens — Devoxx Greece (~Apr)
  Short flight from BCN. Warm in April, affordable, incredible food,
  beaches within reach. Acropolis Museum has a kids area. Very
  stroller-friendly flat areas around Plaka. Wife can do a day trip
  to the coast with the kid. Low stress, low cost.

Tokyo / Yokohama — Japan conferences (May-Dec)
  Counterintuitive with a toddler but Japan is arguably the best
  country in the world for traveling with small kids. Spotlessly clean,
  incredibly safe, trains have priority seating, every mall has nursing
  rooms, convenience stores have everything you need. Yokohama (KubeCon,
  Jul) has the Anpanman Museum — toddler paradise. Tokyo (JJUG, OSS
  Summit) has Ueno Zoo, Shinjuku Gyoen park, and kid-friendly everything.
  Long flight (~13h direct) but worth combining two conferences into one
  extended trip. Best combo: JJUG Fall (~Nov) + OSS Summit (~Dec).

Pasadena / LA — SCaLE (Apr 1-4)
  Disneyland is 30 minutes away. Griffith Observatory, beaches, Santa
  Monica Pier. Perfect April weather. Wife and kid will have no shortage
  of things to do. Direct flights BCN-LAX exist.

Copenhagen — GOTO (~Sep)
  Tivoli Gardens — a toddler dream. September weather is mild. The city
  is built for strollers (flat, bike lanes everywhere, parks on every
  corner). Very safe. Short flight from BCN.


GREAT OPTIONS
-------------

Berlin — Berlin Buzzwords (~Jun)
  Cheap, excellent parks (Tiergarten, Zoo), very stroller-friendly flat
  city. June weather is perfect. Short flight. Affordable food and
  accommodation compared to other EU capitals.

Melbourne — KubeDay + Cloud Native AI Summit (~Oct)
  Melbourne is one of the most family-friendly cities — great parks,
  zoo, aquarium, tram-friendly. The problem is the flight: 22+ hours
  from Barcelona with a 2-year-old. If you're up for it, cluster
  KubeDay + Cloud Native AI Summit in October to justify one long trip.
  Australian spring weather.

Madrid — Codemotion (May 12-13)
  Almost cheating — it's a 1h flight or a train ride. Retiro Park, zoo,
  warm weather. Easiest logistics imaginable. Wife and kid can even go
  home if they want.


SKIP WITH A TODDLER
--------------------

New Orleans (KubeCon NA, Nov) — not much for a 2-year-old, humidity,
  not stroller-friendly
Kansas City (KCDC) — limited family appeal
San Francisco (Data+AI Summit, QCon SF) — steep hills are brutal with
  a stroller, expensive


RECOMMENDED COMBOS
-------------------

1. Japan in November: JJUG CCC Fall (~Nov) + OSS Summit Japan (~Dec)
   = one trip, two conferences, family-friendly paradise. Add 3-4
   tourist days in between. Kyoto day trip with the kid is magical.

2. Greece in April: Devoxx Greece is cheap, short, warm, and Athens
   is a great family destination. No extra travel stress.

3. SCaLE in April: If you want to do the US — Disneyland makes it
   an easy sell at home. Direct BCN-LAX flights available.
