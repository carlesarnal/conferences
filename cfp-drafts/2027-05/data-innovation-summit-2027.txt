SUBMISSION 1: Lakehouse
Title: The Sink Said RUNNING: Is Your CDC-to-Iceberg Pipeline Making Progress?
Suggested track: Data Engineering / Data Platforms
Level: Intermediate

Description:
The sink reports RUNNING while its logs report commits to zero tables. This session traces an insert through Debezium, Kafka on Strimzi, Apicurio Registry and the Iceberg sink to a Trino result, identifying the evidence that proves progress at each boundary.

A recorded control-consumer investigation shows the configuration change that restored commits, without claiming a proven underlying root cause. Local timings illustrate why a commit interval is not a freshness guarantee. Snapshot and current-state queries distinguish receiving a change from reconstructing its effect. Attendees get a source-to-query progress checklist grounded in a reproducible local pipeline.

Three takeaways:
- Check progress from source event to query-visible snapshot.
- Separate connector health from successful data commits.
- Distinguish changelog visibility from current-state reconstruction.

SUBMISSION 2: Data Contracts
Title: Your Model Didn't Change, Your Input Did: Data Contracts for Streaming AI Pipelines
Suggested track: AI Governance / Data Quality
Level: Intermediate

Description:
A feature changes from 30 seconds to 30,000 milliseconds while its Avro type stays long. Deserialization succeeds, but a consumer expecting seconds receives a value a thousand times larger.

We develop a structural/semantic test pair around that feature: an incompatible schema revision exercises Apicurio Registry's reader/writer compatibility policy, while a normalization invariant requires both unit representations to produce the same model input. The walkthrough places each check at its publication, producer or CI boundary. Attendees learn what these controls establish without inferring model quality from successful validation.

Three takeaways:
- Combine Avro compatibility checks with feature-input tests.
- Identify enforcement points and bypass paths.
- Test units and feature meaning without claiming to guarantee model accuracy.
