Title: Classify It Before a Human Sees It: Operating Streaming ML Inference on Kubernetes

Classifying content as it arrives is easy to sketch: a queue, a model, an API. Running it is harder: input spikes, a model slower than the stream, and dashboards that show everything except why predictions are late.

In this session, a live pipeline classifies posts as they arrive: ingestion into Kafka on Strimzi, Spark Structured Streaming running two models, and a Quarkus service serving predictions, with Prometheus and Grafana watching it. We'll push it into backpressure, change micro-batch intervals and watch latency and throughput move, and look at the metrics that separate input lag from slow inference.

Attendees will leave with:
- A reproducible streaming inference pipeline on Kubernetes
- How micro-batch interval, backpressure and model cost trade against each other
- The metrics to alert on before predictions go stale
