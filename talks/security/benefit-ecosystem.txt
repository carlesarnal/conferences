When you give one identity provider to Kafka, its schema registry and the applications around it, credentials get simpler but authorization does not become consistent by itself. This talk maps the boundaries between token validation, Kafka ACLs and registry permissions on CNCF projects (Keycloak, Strimzi, Apicurio Registry), and shows how to check each one. Teams running Kafka on Kubernetes get a reusable checklist instead of discovering the gaps during an audit or an incident.
