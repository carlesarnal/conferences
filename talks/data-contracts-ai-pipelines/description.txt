Title: Your Model Didn't Change, Your Input Did: Data Contracts for Streaming AI Pipelines

An inference service can break with no model change at all: an upstream team renames a field or changes its type, and the consumer quietly gets nulls or stops reading. In event-driven AI pipelines, producers and consumers evolve independently, and nothing stops them from drifting apart.

This session treats the event schema as a contract. With Kafka on Strimzi and Apicurio Registry (CNCF sandbox), we'll register Avro contracts, accept a compatible change and see an incompatible one rejected before it reaches the topic. Then the harder part: where enforcement actually happens (serializers, CI, deployment), what slips through when a producer bypasses it, and why structural compatibility says nothing about whether the values still mean the same thing to the model.

Attendees will leave with:
- A contract workflow for streaming AI pipelines, from schema registration to CI checks
- How Avro, Protobuf and JSON Schema compatibility rules differ for ML features
- A list of the failures schema rules cannot catch, and what to use for them
