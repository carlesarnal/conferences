Title: What the CNCF Sandbox Actually Asks For: Lessons from Bringing Apicurio Registry In

Applying to the CNCF Sandbox looks like a form. In practice it forced us to answer questions we had avoided for years: who decides, who owns the trademark, how a company-started project shows it is vendor-neutral, and what happens to contributors who aren't on the payroll.

This is a maintainer's account of taking Apicurio Registry from a company-led project into the CNCF Sandbox: the governance documents we had to write from scratch, the parts of the contribution process we changed, the review feedback we didn't expect, and what actually changed in the year after acceptance, good and bad.

Attendees will leave with:
- A checklist of what to have in place before applying, based on a real application
- How to make a company-started project credibly vendor-neutral
- What Sandbox status does and doesn't change for adoption, contributors and maintainer workload
