CONTRIBUTOR HAND-OFF CHECKLIST
=============================
Based on the inspected Apicurio PR lifecycle; design exercise, not measured
proof of improved retention. Source links and actual review: ../EVIDENCE.txt.

STATE                    NEXT OWNER       VISIBLE EVIDENCE / NEXT ACTION
Ready for review         Maintainer       Reviewer identified; request review
Changes requested        Contributor      Specific request; push or ask a question
Approved, checks pending CI + maintainer  Current approval; required checks pending
Checks fail              Context-specific Failure evidence; identify who can fix it
Maintainer overdue       Maintainer       Escalation signal; no author inactivity blame
Both sides blocked       Explicit policy  Determine which action unblocks the other

The documented project policy lets the contributor inactivity timer take
precedence when both sides are blocked. Discuss that trade-off rather than
claiming every delayed PR is exempt from stale handling.

EXERCISE
Choose one transition in your project. Can a newcomer tell:
- Who owns the next action?
- What evidence makes the transition valid, and for which commit?
- What to do when that owner is unavailable?
- Whether a bot message describes current state or an obsolete event?
Record one change, an owner and a way to observe whether it helps.
No bot is required: a documented convention can be the simplest implementation.
