Title: Five Years Maintaining Apicurio Registry: What We Got Wrong About Contributors

Most maintainer talks agree that community matters. Fewer say which specific decisions grew a contributor base and which ones quietly pushed people away.

This is a maintainer's account from Apicurio Registry, from a small company-led team to a CNCF Sandbox project: the issues labelled "good first issue" that nobody picked up, the review delays that cost us contributors, the async practices that worked across time zones, and a governance disagreement we handled badly and what we changed afterwards.

Attendees will leave with:
- Concrete practices that turned users into repeat contributors, and the ones that didn't
- How to set review and response expectations a small team can actually keep
- Warning signs of maintainer burnout and what helped
